# Code of conduct

inkan is a small project, and it should be a pleasant place to ask questions, report bugs
and send code, whoever you are and however much experience you have.

So, in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else that's about inkan:

- Be kind and patient. Everyone here was new to something once.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume the worst.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sexual, racist or otherwise demeaning
  language or images. No publishing other people's private information.

If someone makes the space worse for others, their comments may be hidden or removed, and
they may be blocked from the project. That's the maintainer's call.

To report something, write to vxnsin@icloud.com. It stays between you and the maintainer.

This follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
